\title{Does Stop-Gradient Still Matter Under SIGReg?\\
\large A Small-Scale Ablation of JEPA Collapse Prevention}
\author{}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Joint-embedding predictive architectures avoid representation collapse through a
collection of heuristics: stop-gradient, exponential-moving-average target encoders, and
masking design. LeJEPA introduces SIGReg and reports that it removes the need for these
heuristics. We test, at small scale on CIFAR-10, whether that holds as training duration
grows, and whether cheap collapse diagnostics detect degradation earlier than a linear
probe does. \textbf{Results section to be completed once runs finish.}
\end{abstract}
