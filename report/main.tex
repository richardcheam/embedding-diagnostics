\title{Can You Trust a Self-Supervised Scenario Embedding?\\
\large Diagnosing Representation Degeneration in Masked JEPA Training}
\author{}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Scenario mining for ADAS validation embeds millions of unlabelled driving clips and asks
geometric questions of them. That rests entirely on label-free diagnostics, so we ask which
of them can be trusted. Collapse-prevention mechanisms --- stop-gradient, EMA, SIGReg and a
disposable projector --- are used as instruments to manufacture known degeneration, including
a control designed to fail outright, across seven conditions and five paired seeds on
CIFAR-10 and BDD100K.

\ours{} Our central result is a dissociation. The control degenerates on every geometric
measure: total variance $10^{-4}$ against a healthy $107.7$, mean pairwise cosine exactly
$1.0000$, RankMe $1.06$ out of 192 dimensions. Yet measured with a correctly optimised linear
probe it remains nearly as decodable as a condition that genuinely trained --- on BDD100K's
\texttt{timeofday} attribute it scores $0.914$ against a healthy $0.927$ and a majority floor
of $0.483$, and on balanced accuracy it is indistinguishable from the weakest
genuinely-training arm on all three attributes. Geometric degeneration does not imply loss of
decodable information.

\ours{} A second dissociation sits inside the semantic endpoints themselves. Linear
decodability survives contraction; nearest-neighbour structure does not. Retrieval P@10 falls
from $0.675$ to $0.566$ against a $0.429$ chance floor, a gap that clears across-seed noise
where the probe comparison does not. For scenario mining this is the consequential half: the
application \emph{is} retrieval, and it is the endpoint that degrades while the commonly
reported one does not.

\ours{} We also report a reproducible inversion between two measures both described in the
literature as effective rank. The covariance participation ratio reads $25.45$ on the
contracted control against $10.99$ on the healthy baseline --- paired 95\% interval
$[-32.70, -15.36]$, in the wrong direction --- because it subtracts the mean and so removes
precisely the degeneracy, while RankMe, computed from raw singular values, correctly reports
$1.06$. The underlying algebra is known; the reproducible inversion in real training is what
we contribute.

\interp{} This report also records a claim we withdrew. An earlier version argued that
standardized probing is blind to collapse while unstandardized probing detects it. That
rested on a defective probe: on features of magnitude $10^{-3}$, lbfgs satisfies its
gradient-norm stopping criterion before taking a useful step, returns the majority-class
prediction, and reports chance while raising no warning. Recomputing from saved encoders
moved the affected endpoint from $0.483$ to $0.914$ while every other condition moved by at
most $0.01$. Section~\ref{sec:provenance} records what was fixed before the experiments, what
was withdrawn, and what was chosen after seeing results.
\end{abstract}
