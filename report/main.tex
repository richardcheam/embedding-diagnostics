\begin{abstract}
\ours{} This diagnostic study combines controlled JEPA training, pretrained
EmbeddingGemma interventions, learned versus post-hoc compression, numerical
neighbourhood checks and an original-source COCO image--caption evaluation.
The current qualified BDD analysis retains 2,000 training and 983 validation
rows after excluding 17 source-confirmed generated graphics. Historical records
are preserved. Corrected training probes, fixed-sample pretrained comparisons
and conditional COCO intervals have different uncertainty scopes.
\interp{} Geometry, label decodability, neighbour identity, recorded-positive
hits and multiple-caption coverage measure different properties. Familiar
compression behavior is distinct from these diagnostic comparisons. No universal
quality predictor, novel compression method or independent encoder replication
is established. A Jina replication attempt failed its CPU FP32 contract before
any forward; it supplies no embedding-quality evidence. Experiments are closed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Design and evidence scope}
\ours{} Phase A/B used one Trainer and seven collapse-prevention strategies,
including EMA, stop-gradient and SIGReg variants with and without a projector.
The masked prediction architecture follows I-JEPA \citep{assran2023ijepa}; the
regularizer follows SIGReg \citep{lejepa2025}.
Phase B used five paired seeds on a fixed driving-image evaluation split.
The corrected probes supersede defective fixed-C/raw-feature fits: the earlier
near-majority control score was a solver artifact. Some corrected raw fits still
reach the iteration limit. Phase-A probe endpoints remain provisional.
\interp{} The SIGReg loss passed reference checks; the surrounding masked
prediction system is not LeJEPA and supports no system-level reproduction claim.

\ours{} Phase C used pinned \texttt{google/embeddinggemma-2}, revision
\texttt{914f7f89142e33e77833254d9c9b90c3cef7303b}, text plus vision with audio
excluded, CPU FP32, masked mean pooling and L2 normalization. C1 uses five
controlled feature-space interventions; C2 compares native 768d, learned MRL
512/256/128 and centered training-fitted PCA at the same budgets. Shared
training-fitted projections were verified for C1 rank loss and C2 PCA.
C3 separates vector storage precision from similarity arithmetic, with exact
search and one frozen mean-offset stress. No model quantization or ANN was tested.

\section{What do geometry diagnostics reveal and miss?}
\established{} Total variance is centered spread. Mean pairwise cosine describes
directional agreement. RankMe \citep{garrido2023rankme} uses the raw singular spectrum; participation
ratio uses centered covariance eigenvalues. Positive scaling preserves cosine
ordering and both rank ratios, while variance contracts quadratically. A common
offset preserves centered variance. These responses follow from definitions.

\ours{} In corrected Phase B, time-of-day probe accuracy is approximately
91.37\% for the contracted control and 92.71\% for the EMA reference; P@10 is
approximately 56.57\% versus 67.47\%. Decodable labels survive pronounced
geometric degeneration, but the retrieval endpoints differ. Five paired
training-seed Student-t intervals and convergence qualifications are retained
in the public page's training appendix and recorded reprobe tables.

\ours{} Qualified pretrained C1 reproduces the expected scaling and offset
invariances of standardized probing. Severe isotropic noise raises RankMe while
weather P@10 approaches chance. \interp{} These are manufactured mathematical
interventions, not naturally observed training trajectories. Raw versus centered
rank can disagree without either furnishing a universal quality verdict.
Absolute variance thresholds from unnormalized Phase-B vectors do not transfer.

\section{How does compression affect different utility endpoints?}
\ours{} The qualified seven-condition C2 comparison retains much of the measured
utility at MRL 512/256; 128 has larger costs on several endpoints. Neither MRL
nor PCA is uniformly better. PCA 128 exceeds MRL 128 on weather balanced accuracy,
while MRL 128 has slightly higher weather P@10. The full 983-row tables and
per-class support are linked from the accompanying synthesis.
\interp{} Lower nominal dimension does not imply proportional semantic loss.
This is familiar compression behavior, with task-specific diagnostic comparisons;
centering and coordinate construction prevent isolating mechanism causes.
Raw RankMe and participation ratio remain distinct from dimension-normalized
capacity fractions. Three dimensions do not validate a general quality predictor.

\section{When does numerical perturbation change neighbour identity?}
\ours{} Native FP16 gallery storage changes five of 983 top-10 sets; native INT8
gallery storage changes 60. FP32 normalization/scoring changes none. The frozen
mean-offset FP16 stress changes 195 sets despite smaller absolute score error.
All native FP16 changes fall in the lowest reference-margin bin; INT8 gallery
changes distribute 55/5/0/0 across four deterministic bins.
\established{} Reference margins exceeding bounded score perturbations can
sufficiently guarantee set preservation mathematically. \ours{} Prospective
reconstruction-based conditions cover 576 native FP16-gallery queries with no
empirical exception, and cover no INT8 or stressed queries.
\interp{} This is not rigorous floating-point certification; retrospective
observed errors must not be confused with prospective bounds.

\ours{} Among 195 stressed identity changes, weather/scene/time P@10 is unchanged
for 131/119/149 queries. \interp{} Entering/departing label counts distinguish
attribute turnover from identity turnover without arbitrary pairings. Unmeasured
instance semantics remain unknown. FP64 reconstructed working arrays do not
establish production storage/search performance.

\section{Does preserving a positive preserve ranking and caption coverage?}
\ours{} The accepted COCO study sampled 1,000 original-release validation image
groups with five human-authored captions each, using the same encoder and exactly
native 768d, MRL 256d and MRL 128d. Prefixes are L2 renormalized. Text-to-image
uses query-role text; image-to-text uses document-role text. Directional differences
cannot isolate modality asymmetry. Independent evaluation sampling is not an
independent encoder replication.
\input{sections/final_tables}

\ours{} At 128d, image-to-text Hit@10 declines 2.50 percentage points, Hit@1
10.60 points, and fraction of five captions retrieved at ten 11.34 points.
At 256d the differences are $-0.30$, $-0.70$ and $-1.74$ points.
Top-10 identity overlap is approximately .84 and .65 at 256d and 128d in both
directions. \interp{} At least one recorded positive is a weaker endpoint than
first-rank recovery or multiple-caption coverage; these are not interchangeable
with coarse image-category P@10.

\ours{} Conditional paired 95\% intervals for Hit@10 differences are:
text-to-image 256, $-0.20\ [-0.54,+0.14]$ points; image-to-text 256,
$-0.30\ [-1.10,+0.50]$; text-to-image 128, $-3.34\ [-4.06,-2.62]$;
image-to-text 128, $-2.50\ [-3.60,-1.40]$.
\interp{} Intervals crossing zero are not equivalence claims.
\ours{} A native circular pairing control preserves all vector geometry but
reduces Hit@10 to 1.22\% and .20\% in the two directions.
\interp{} Marginal distributions cannot establish paired alignment. Category
and paired utility broadly decline under stronger compression here; there is
no strong reversal between preserved coarse utility and destroyed pairing.

\section{Source correction, uncertainty and limitations}
\ours{} Seventeen confirmed generated graphics inherited from the enriched BDD
validation fragment were excluded in a separately frozen post-audit sensitivity,
as both queries and gallery candidates. All 2,000 training rows remain unchanged.
The 983-row validation analysis is current; original caches and campaigns remain
historical. Verified source-fragment byte continuity does not authenticate original
BDD images or labels. Tunnel, gas stations and foggy have zero validation support;
six pilot appended-fragment IDs remain separately unresolved.

\interp{} Phase-B paired Student-t intervals describe five training seeds on one
fixed split, not population uncertainty. Pretrained BDD effects are descriptive
fixed-sample measurements. COCO's 5,000 paired parent-group bootstrap draws hold
the gallery fixed and carry all five captions together. They do not measure
encoder training variability or independent sampling of the gallery.
Original COCO acquisition, annotation joins and content inspections support
original-release provenance; they do not resolve unknown pretraining overlap,
residual near/event duplicates or incomplete recorded relevance.

\ours{} Jina-CLIP-v2 remains an incomplete replication: constructor resolution
was repaired, then the full-wrapper CPU FP32 parameter contract failed before
any forward. Both failed attempts, freezes and reports remain intact. The exact
parameter-level cause was not persisted. No Jina quality or compression claim
is supported, and no further repair is undertaken.

\section{Conclusion and evidence access}
\interp{} The study supports reading diagnostic definitions, numerical behavior
and task endpoints together before interpreting surprising geometry. It supports
no new universal quality predictor, novel compression algorithm, deployment
performance claim or independent encoder replication. \ours{} The Jina replication failed its CPU FP32 contract before any forward and
supports no quality or compression claim. Experimental scope is closed;
maintenance and documented corrections remain possible. Audio--visual post-training
belongs to a separate project.
