\section{Provenance of the analysis}
\label{sec:provenance}

\subsection{Fixed before training campaigns}
\ours{} The seven training conditions, final-checkpoint endpoints, independent
evaluation split and five paired Student-t contrasts were fixed before Phase A.
The twice-standard-deviation heuristic was withdrawn before the first run
(\texttt{96184e2}); later starred cells and early three-verdict panels carry no
inferential weight. The corrected Phase-B probe protocol was selected after
results existed and is not a new preregistration of those data.

\subsection{Withdrawn measurement claim}
\ours{} The earlier claim that the unstandardized probe detects degeneration
where the standardized probe does not is withdrawn as refuted. On tiny features,
lbfgs could satisfy its gradient tolerance before fitting and return a majority
prediction without a warning. Correcting regularization selection and solver
settings moved Phase-B control time-of-day accuracy from .4832 to .9137.
Convergence limits remain recorded. Phase-A probe endpoints were not corrected
because that campaign saved no encoders; they remain provisional, with no rerun
undertaken in this closure.

\subsection{Confirmatory scope and exploratory observations}
\ours{} C1 hypotheses were frozen before main extraction, but attribute-specific
severity optima and metric orderings remain exploratory. C2 was a predeclared
analysis on the already examined C1 sample, not an independent replication.
C3's implementation and condition freeze preceded endpoint computation.
The source-exclusion sensitivity was separately frozen after discovery of the
17 generated graphics; it is a post-audit sensitivity, not an independent sample
or retroactive preregistration. Its 983-row results are current qualified evidence.
The original 3,000-row cache, results, exclusions, and source qualifications remain
preserved. Six pilot-fragment IDs remain separately unresolved.

\ours{} COCO source/sample/input identity was frozen before inference and complete
canonical cache hashes were bound before endpoints. Native 768d and learned
256/128d were the fixed grid. Its original-source evaluation sampling is independent
of BDD, with the same encoder; unknown pretraining overlap remains.
Conditional bootstrap intervals retain the fixed gallery and parent-caption units.
The frozen Jina attempts produced no forwards or semantic endpoints. Neither
supplies independent encoder replication.

\subsection{Display provenance and closure}
The final public preview and LaTeX table read checksummed accepted records using
\texttt{viz/evidence-lock.json}, whose source baseline is \texttt{e902aa0}.
Record checksums are verified before display. No scientific endpoints, inference
or source images were recomputed during synthesis. Existing training plots
summarize corrected committed endpoints with their established paired protocol.
Detailed freezes and diagnostic histories are linked in the evidence index.
Experiments are closed; preservation, maintenance and transparent corrections
remain possible. Publication or deployment requires separate authorization.
