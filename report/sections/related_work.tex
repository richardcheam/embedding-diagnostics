\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Joint-embedding predictive architectures.} I-JEPA
\citep{assran2023ijepa} predicts representations of masked image blocks from a visible
context block, with an EMA target encoder and stop-gradient on the target branch. V-JEPA 2
\citep{vjepa2_2025} extends the approach to video and action-conditioned prediction, and
reports limitations at longer prediction horizons.

\paragraph{Collapse prevention.} BYOL \citep{grill2020byol} showed that a predictor plus an
EMA target can avoid collapse without negatives. SimSiam \citep{chen2021simsiam} showed
stop-gradient alone is sufficient in that setting. Both are empirical findings about
optimization dynamics rather than guarantees.

\paragraph{Distributional regularization.} LeJEPA \citep{lejepa2025} introduces Sketched
Isotropic Gaussian Regularization, which constrains embeddings toward an isotropic Gaussian
by matching random one-dimensional projections against a standard normal, and reports that
this removes the need for the heuristics above.
